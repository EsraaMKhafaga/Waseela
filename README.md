🌿 Waseela

📝 About The Project
Waseela is a mobile application designed to support older adults who live alone by helping them manage their daily routines, stay connected, and access help when needed.
The app provides features such as daily check-ins, one-tap emergency alerts, medication and appointment reminders, calls and social activities, and trusted-contact notifications. It also gives family members or trusted contacts a simple way to stay informed about the older adult’s safety without creating a feeling of constant monitoring.
Waseela aims to create a safe, simple, and supportive digital experience that promotes independence while keeping loved ones connected and reassured.
